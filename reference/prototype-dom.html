PRICING

You pay a rate per hour, multiplied by the hours you book, multiplied by the slot's current surge. Nothing compounds and nothing carries over.

The rate is flat per hour. Six hours costs exactly six times one hour — no bulk discount, no long-hold premium. Surge is capped at 2× and only rises while a slot's queue is deep.

HOW SURGE MOVES

Every person who joins a slot's queue pushes that slot's surge up; every rental that expires without a replacement pulls it back toward base. Slot 03 has no queue right now, so it sells at base.

HOW TO READ THE CHART
The y-axis is dollars per hour. The x-axis is clock time. One candle is one hour of asking price.
Filled green: the hour closed above where it opened.
Hollow red: the hour closed below where it opened.
Navy dot: a completed sale. Amount paid is in the trade tape.
The dashed line is that slot's base rate. Above it means busy; sitting on it means available at base.
